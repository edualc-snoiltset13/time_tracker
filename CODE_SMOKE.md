Date: 2026-08-06

Time Tracker is a cross-platform Flutter time tracking and invoicing app for freelancers, consultants, and small teams, targeting Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, and web.
It manages clients, projects, and to-do tasks, supports real-time and manual time entries, records expenses (including mileage), and generates professional PDF invoices with reporting charts.
Data is stored locally in SQLite via the Drift ORM (tables for clients, projects, time entries, expenses, invoices, todos, and company settings), with Provider for state management and fl_chart, pdf, and printing for visualization and invoice output.
